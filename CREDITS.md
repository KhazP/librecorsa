# LibreCorsa: credits & licensing

LibreCorsa is an independent, non-commercial, open-source web simulator built with Next.js, React, Three.js, and the Web Audio API.

### Suzuki Liana (`public/cars/liana/`, `src/lib/sim/road/`)

* **Physics**: from `"Physics reworked for Top Gear Liana 1.6 GLX"` ([https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/physics-reworked-for-top-gear-liana-1-6-glx.28636/](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/physics-reworked-for-top-gear-liana-1-6-glx.28636/)) by **alekabul** ([https://www.overtake.gg/members/alekabul.294399/](https://www.overtake.gg/members/alekabul.294399/)), an Assetto Corsa physics rework of the Liana, used with the author's permission. Its engine, gearing, differential, suspension, tyre, aero, brake and electronics data are mapped onto LibreCorsa's rigid-body vehicle model in `src/lib/sim/road/SuzukiLianaConfig.ts`; each value carries a `[spec]` tag there. The front MacPherson struts are solved as double wishbones with a virtual upper arm, and the AC v3 tyre's linear load sensitivity is tabulated for the tyre model. Checked against the mod's own laps of Dunsfold by `scripts/verify-liana.ts`.
* **3D model**: the Suzuki Liana from *Forza Motorsport 4* (Turn 10 Studios / Microsoft), as converted for Assetto Corsa in 2015 (`suzuki_liana`, [http://assetto-db.com/car/suzuki_liana](http://assetto-db.com/car/suzuki_liana)); the converter is not named in the mod. Its mesh and texture names (`suz_liana_02_*_lod0`, `nodamage_lod0.dds`) are Forza's. **Not licensed for redistribution**: kept out of public releases until there is a cleared replacement (see *Rights holders*). Changes for the simulator (`scripts/cars/port_ac_car.py`): the KN5 meshes converted to glTF 2.0 on the ported-car rig (`WHEEL_*` hubs with rim and tyre, `STEER` pivot), the light-blue skin baked into the paint, textures downscaled and re-encoded.

### MTRacing 496R (`public/cars/mtr-496r/`, `src/lib/sim/proto/`)

* From `"MTRacing 496R"` v0.9 by **munyuturbo** ([https://www.overtake.gg/members/munyuturbo.1202939/](https://www.overtake.gg/members/munyuturbo.1202939/)), an Assetto Corsa car, used in LibreCorsa with the author's permission. The model, textures and sounds stay the author's; they are not covered by the MIT licence.
* **Physics**: its engine, gearing, differential, suspension (with heave springs), tyre, aero, brake and traction-control data are mapped onto LibreCorsa's rigid-body vehicle model in `src/lib/sim/proto/Mtr496rConfig.ts`; each value carries a `[spec]` tag there. Checked by `scripts/verify-mtr-496r.ts`.
* **3D model**: changes for the simulator (`scripts/cars/port_ac_car.py`): the KN5 meshes converted to glTF 2.0 on the ported-car rig (`WHEEL_*` hubs with rim, brake disc and tyre, `STEER` pivot with the wheel's screen and shift lights as nodes of their own), the "MTR Green 04" skin baked into the paint, textures downscaled and re-encoded. The mod's 20 skins are the car's liveries (`public/cars/mtr-496r/skins/`): each one's paint, door decals and sunstrip, baked the same way.

### 2026 Pirelli tyre sidewall (`public/cars/shared/tyres/pirelli-2026-sidewall-cx.webp`)

* Ported from `"2026 Pirelli Tire Pack"` by **Cxjovich** ([https://www.overtake.gg/members/cxjovich.4261212/](https://www.overtake.gg/members/cxjovich.4261212/)), made for the Formula Hybrid X 2026 in Assetto Corsa ([https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/pirelli-tire-pack-for-formula-hybrid-x-2026.81209/](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/pirelli-tire-pack-for-formula-hybrid-x-2026.81209/)), licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/) and used in LibreCorsa with the author's permission. The artwork stays the author's; it is not covered by the MIT licence. Changes for the simulator (`scripts/cars/port_cxjovich_sidewall.py`): the outer sidewall's logo row cut from the pack's slick (C2) and wet tyre textures, re-projected from the tyre strip onto a disc round the axle, and packed into one lossless WebP as the rubber albedo plus slick and wet logo masks. The tyre shader of the F1 cars from 2018 on (`src/lib/render/pirelli-sidewall.ts`) draws it on the sidewalls and tints the logos in the compound colour. The pack's intermediate and full-wet temperature curves (`FR_Temp_Comp_6/7.lut`) are transcribed into the F1 cars' rain tyres (`F1_RAIN_TYRE_PACK` in `src/lib/sim/wet-tyres.ts`), and its wear curves (`F/R_Wear_Comp_6/7.lut`) set how much slower the full wet wears than the intermediate. The pack's tread textures, normal maps and other files are not included.

### Car sounds (`public/cars/<id>/audio/`)

Ported from Assetto Corsa FMOD Studio `.bank` (`FSB5`) sound mods into LibreCorsa's Web Audio multi-grid sample format (`scripts/audio/port_ac_bank.py`):

* **F1 2026 (`vf26`, `f12026-car` → `public/cars/f12026/audio/`)**: Ported from **F1 2026 Sound Pack** (Aston Martin 2026 sound bank; author not listed in archive or bank metadata).

The bank is built from Assetto Corsa's own samples (Kunos Simulazioni; e.g. `BMW_E30_1987_backfire_int_1`, `ALFA_156_2007_backfire_int_2`), which are **not licensed for redistribution**.

* **Mercedes-AMG GT3 Evo (`amg-gt3` → `public/gt3/audio/`)**: the existing GT3 bank, ported from TRR's "Mercedes-AMG GT3 EVO" Assetto Corsa mod with the author's permission (`scripts/gt3/port_sounds.py`).

## 3. Open Source Frameworks & Tools

* **Next.js** — Vercel (MIT)
* **Three.js** — Ricardo Cabello / Mr.doob and contributors (MIT)
* **React** — Meta Platforms, Inc. (MIT)
* **Tailwind CSS** — Tailwind Labs (MIT)
* **Framer Motion** — Motion Division (MIT)
* **Lucide Icons** — Lucide Contributors (ISC)
* **@takram/three-atmosphere, @takram/three-clouds, @takram/three-geospatial** — Shota Matsuda / Takram (MIT): the physically based sky and volumetric clouds of the Medium and High graphics presets (`src/lib/render/physical-sky.ts`). The clouds' blue-noise texture is the project's own (`scripts/generate-blue-noise.ts`), not the library's default.
* **global_racetrajectory_optimization** — TUM Institute of Automotive Technology (LGPL-3.0): minimum-time racing lines that seed each circuit's F1 and GT3 lines. Used offline only, not bundled.
* **racetrack-database** — TUM Institute of Automotive Technology (LGPL-3.0): centrelines and track widths, used offline to cross-check the widths measured on the circuit scans. Not bundled.
* **Design references** (no code or assets used): the kerb as a height ramp with a ripple, as in TORCS / Speed Dreams, and the per-surface table (friction, bump, vibration, valid track) as in Assetto Corsa's `surfaces.ini`. The overtaking-aid rules by era (`src/lib/sim/overtaking-rules.ts`: the 1 s gap measured once at the detection point, lapped cars counting, DRS from the leader's third lap, the same rules for the AI) follow the FIA sporting regulations, cross-checked against [**assetto-corsa-fia-drs-rules**](https://github.com/Zhaoyi-Fan/assetto-corsa-fia-drs-rules) by **Zhaoyi Fan** (MIT).
