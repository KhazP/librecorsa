# LibreCorsa: credits & licensing

LibreCorsa is an independent, non-commercial, open-source web simulator built with Next.js, React, Three.js, and the Web Audio API.

### Suzuki Liana (`public/cars/liana/`, `src/lib/sim/road/`)

* **Physics**: from `"Physics reworked for Top Gear Liana 1.6 GLX"` ([https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/physics-reworked-for-top-gear-liana-1-6-glx.28636/](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/physics-reworked-for-top-gear-liana-1-6-glx.28636/)) by **alekabul** ([https://www.overtake.gg/members/alekabul.294399/](https://www.overtake.gg/members/alekabul.294399/)), an Assetto Corsa physics rework of the Liana, used with the author's permission. Its engine, gearing, differential, suspension, tyre, aero, brake and electronics data are mapped onto LibreCorsa's rigid-body vehicle model in `src/lib/sim/road/SuzukiLianaConfig.ts`; each value carries a `[spec]` tag there. The front MacPherson struts are solved as double wishbones with a virtual upper arm, and the AC v3 tyre's linear load sensitivity is tabulated for the tyre model. Checked against the mod's own laps of Dunsfold by `scripts/verify-liana.ts`.
* **3D model**: the Suzuki Liana from *Forza Motorsport 4* (Turn 10 Studios / Microsoft), as converted for Assetto Corsa in 2015 (`suzuki_liana`, [http://assetto-db.com/car/suzuki_liana](http://assetto-db.com/car/suzuki_liana)); the converter is not named in the mod. Its mesh and texture names (`suz_liana_02_*_lod0`, `nodamage_lod0.dds`) are Forza's. **Not licensed for redistribution**, and its owners haven't been found: alekabul, who gave permission for the physics, says the model isn't his and that he couldn't find them either (see *Rights holders*). Changes for the simulator (`scripts/cars/port_ac_car.py`): the KN5 meshes converted to glTF 2.0 on the ported-car rig (`WHEEL_*` hubs with rim and tyre, `STEER` pivot), the light-blue skin baked into the paint, textures downscaled and re-encoded.

### 2025 Pirelli tyres of the 2026 car (`public/cars/shared/tyres/pirelli-2025-ps-*.webp`)

* Ported from `"2025 Pirelli tyres"` by **Purple Sector** ([https://www.overtake.gg/members/purple-sector.3636642/](https://www.overtake.gg/members/purple-sector.3636642/); their newer work is on Patreon, [https://www.patreon.com/cw/PurpleSector](https://www.patreon.com/cw/PurpleSector)), used in LibreCorsa with the author's permission. The artwork stays the author's; it is not covered by the MIT licence. Changes for the simulator (`scripts/cars/port_purple_sector_tyres.py`): the five compound textures split into the rubber (the slicks' and the wet's, and the intermediate's with its tread) and the printed art of the outer sidewall as a coverage mask, so one texture serves every compound; the maker's lines moulded into the sidewalls and a handwritten team mark with its barcode sticker painted out and filled from the rubber beside them; re-encoded as WebP. The 2026 car's tyre shader (`src/lib/render/tyre-wrap.ts`) wraps them round the whole tyre, both sidewalls and the tread, and prints the art in the pack's colour for the compound fitted. The pack's wet has the slick's tread, so the wet tyre has no tread pattern. The pack's normal maps and blurred textures are not included.

### 2026 Pirelli tyre sidewall (`public/cars/shared/tyres/pirelli-2026-sidewall-cx.webp`)

* Ported from `"2026 Pirelli Tire Pack"` by **Cxjovich** ([https://www.overtake.gg/members/cxjovich.4261212/](https://www.overtake.gg/members/cxjovich.4261212/)), made for the Formula Hybrid X 2026 in Assetto Corsa ([https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/pirelli-tire-pack-for-formula-hybrid-x-2026.81209/](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/pirelli-tire-pack-for-formula-hybrid-x-2026.81209/)), licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/) and used in LibreCorsa with the author's permission. The artwork stays the author's; it is not covered by the MIT licence. Changes for the simulator (`scripts/cars/port_cxjovich_sidewall.py`): the outer sidewall's logo row cut from the pack's slick (C2) and wet tyre textures, re-projected from the tyre strip onto a disc round the axle, and packed into one lossless WebP as the rubber albedo plus slick and wet logo masks. The tyre shader of the ported F1 cars from 2018 on (`src/lib/render/pirelli-sidewall.ts`) draws it on the sidewalls and tints the logos in the compound colour. The 2026 car wears Purple Sector's tyres (above) and draws this sidewall only when those textures cannot be fetched; no ported F1 car is in the game at the moment, so that is the only place it shows today. The pack's intermediate and full-wet temperature curves (`FR_Temp_Comp_6/7.lut`) are transcribed into the F1 cars' rain tyres (`F1_RAIN_TYRE_PACK` in `src/lib/sim/wet-tyres.ts`), and its wear curves (`F/R_Wear_Comp_6/7.lut`) set how much slower the full wet wears than the intermediate. The pack's tread textures, normal maps and other files are not included.
