# Glossary: German (de)

## Style
- Address the player as "du" (lowercase in running text), never "Sie". Avoid addressing the player in short labels.
- Buttons and actions: infinitive ("Speichern", "Fortsetzen", "Beenden", "Beitreten"). Labels are nouns.
- Apostrophe: always the typographic ’ (U+2019), never ASCII '. Rarely needed in German (e.g. "Alp’s" is wrong anyway; use "von Alp").
- Quotes: „…“ (low-high German quotes: U+201E and U+201C). Ellipsis as a single "…". No space before : ; ! ?
- Compound nouns written as one word or hyphenated per Duden ("Rundenzeit", "KI-Rennen", "Punkt-zu-Punkt-Strecke").
- Stay in English: car names, circuit names, LibreCorsa, DRS, ERS, ABS, TC, MFD, HUD, FFB, km/h, mph, rpm (U/min is not used), S1/S2/S3, P1/P2, keyboard key names (Esc, Shift, Enter, Space, F8, Ctrl+V; the German keyboard labels "Strg" and "Umschalt" are NOT used, keep Ctrl+V as given), controller button names (Cross, Circle, A, B, L3, R2, LB).
- Normal usage in German sim racing: "Force Feedback" (English), "Setup", "Ghost", "Safety Car", "Pit"/"Boxengasse" both used (use "Boxengasse"), "Pole-Position". "Kerb" is the common sim word for Curbs; use "Kerb".
- Compounds with "Reifen" are closed: Reifenverschleiß, Reifendruck.
